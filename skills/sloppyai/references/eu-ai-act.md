# AI use and the EU AI Act

sloppy.ai ships a skill for your coding agent and a local CLI with fixed checks.
The coding agent produces AI-assisted findings and edits using its own model and
permissions. Read the evidence, inspect the diff and rerun relevant checks before
shipping changes. Check your agent and hook settings before authorizing any
external processing of project files.

## Review the actual use

Record which component uses AI, what it does, who provides or operates it, who
sees its outputs and where the system or outputs are used. If EU scope is unclear,
record the facts that need confirmation. Check intended purpose against Article 5,
Article 6 and Annexes I and III where the feature warrants it. Article 2(12) limits
the scope for freely licensed open-source AI systems and has exceptions for
high-risk systems and those covered by Articles 5 or 50.
[EU AI Act, Articles 2–6 and Annexes I and III](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng)

Use Article 50 to review applicable interactions and outputs. Verify that required
information is clear and accessible by the first interaction or exposure.

| Use | Evidence to check |
| --- | --- |
| An AI system interacts directly with people | Open the entry point and check whether the provider makes the AI interaction clear. Consider the exception when the AI interaction is already obvious in context. |
| A provider's system generates synthetic audio, images, video or text | Check whether representative outputs carry detectable, machine-readable marks after export. Consider technical feasibility and the exceptions for standard editing or edits that preserve the substance of the input. |
| A deployer uses emotion recognition or biometric categorisation | Check the Article 5 restrictions before reviewing what the people exposed to the system are told about its operation. |
| A deployer publishes AI-generated or manipulated deepfakes | Verify disclosure at first exposure. Assess resemblance and whether the content could falsely appear authentic. For evidently creative, fictional or similar works, check that the disclosure suits the work. |
| A deployer publishes AI-generated or manipulated text to inform the public on a matter of public interest | Check the disclosure or evidence of human review or editorial control and an accountable publisher under the editorial exception. |

Preserve available provenance through editing and export. For each finding, record
the feature or asset, the article, the evidence and what still needs confirmation.
A static landing page promoting a skill can be reviewed for its published media.
For an AI chat embedded in that page, also test the visitor's first interaction.
Use those observations as evidence for the responsible team's assessment.
[European Commission Article 50 guidelines](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations)

## Using the skill at work

Support the AI literacy of staff who operate AI on your behalf. Show them how to
check the agent's evidence, what project data they can share and who reviews edits
before release. Adapt this guidance to the team's work and record what was covered.
[EU AI Act, Article 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng)
