# Media, documents, rights and distribution

Inspect the actual rendered or exported artifact using suitable available tools.
Check reading order, line breaks, clipping, image identity, visual text, diagram
relationships, chart data, slide/page composition and export fidelity.

For video/audio inspect temporal continuity, narrative contribution, measured
speaker timing, pronunciation, intelligibility, clipping, music balance and
agreement between narration, visible words and imagery. Do not assume generated
footage depicts a real event or that a technical-looking chart has real data.

Track actual sources, licences, permissions and required credit for footage,
images, music, fonts and code. Ownership of generated output and permission to use
incorporated material are separate questions. Legal conclusions depend on the
jurisdiction and facts; preserve human creative contribution records when relevant.

Check current platform rules for realistic synthetic-media disclosure, repetitive
content, reused material and scaled publishing. Do not invent a blanket AI ban.

M and R entries are contextual references. The static CLI does not open binary
media, execute a renderer or assess external assets. Its inputDigest covers only
the listed static manifest and configuration. For excluded assets, record the
actual file hashes and tool observations in a separate current evidence record;
do not present a static-source digest as freshness proof for those assets.
