## Workflow inspiration

The project grew from a discussion of artifact quality and five user-supplied
reference screenshots. The 102 labels are retained for traceability with original
explanations and contextual treatment. No screenshot, portrait, private source path,
social-media transcript or third-party article is distributed.

The original screenshots showed lists by Instagram creators charles.unemployed
and murphmaxxing. Their labels are references, not claims of endorsement or a
licence to republish the source images.

[gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) informed product framing, interaction
state coverage, scoped evidence, meaningful tests, developer experience and
bounded verification workflows. This implementation and its instructions were
written independently. No gstack engine, prompt file, browser runtime or source
fixture is bundled. gstack is MIT licensed and has its own
[third-party notices](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/NOTICE.md),
including upstream material with other terms. Any future code/text reuse needs
its own provenance and licence review.

Normative references, provider guidance, studies and community observations are
indexed in skills/craftcheck/catalog/sources.json. Referencing a source does not
make every stylistic opinion a standard or establish empirical prevalence.

## Bundled JavaScript libraries

The portable helpers bundle their parsing and schema-validation dependencies.
Their exact versions and complete licence notices are generated from the locked
build inputs into skills/craftcheck/scripts/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md. That file
travels with copied skill installations and release packages.
